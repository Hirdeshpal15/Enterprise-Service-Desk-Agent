# VPN Access Policy

## Purpose

The VPN service provides secure remote access to company resources.

## Requirements

- Employees must use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Only company-managed devices may connect to the VPN.
- VPN access must be approved by the employee's manager.

## Troubleshooting

If VPN connection fails:

1. Verify internet connectivity.
2. Confirm MFA is functioning.
3. Restart the VPN client.
4. Contact the Service Desk if the issue persists.

## Security

Sharing VPN credentials is strictly prohibited.
