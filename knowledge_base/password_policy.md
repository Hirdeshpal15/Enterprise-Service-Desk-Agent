# Password Management Policy

## Password Reset

Employees may reset passwords through the Self-Service Password Reset portal.

## Requirements

- MFA verification is required.
- Passwords must contain:
  - Minimum 12 characters
  - Uppercase letter
  - Lowercase letter
  - Number
  - Special character

## Account Lockout

Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts.

## Service Desk

The Service Desk cannot directly view or disclose passwords.
